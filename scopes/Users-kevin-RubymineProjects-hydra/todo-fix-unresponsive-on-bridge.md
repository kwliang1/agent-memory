**Slack react-settle defect (found + fixed 2026-07-28, was pre-existing in the APPROVED PR):** on Slack `react` is a *success-returning no-op* (`daemon/bridge-dispatch.ts:188` short-circuits; `slack-gateway.ts:451-454` no-ops because bot reactions push-notify), so `settlePendingOnReact` disarmed the guard on an ack Kevin never sees — the guard defeating itself on the only platform in use.
