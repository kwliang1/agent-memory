Kevin asked me to "be more concise in your recommendation" after a series of long, multi-section Slack replies during the balance-check discussion.

**Why:** The analysis/investigation can be thorough, but the *recommendation* should be tight — a few sentences, no preamble, no restating the tradeoffs he already saw me reason through.

**How to apply:** Do the rigorous work, but when stating what to do, lead with the recommendation in 1-3 sentences and stop. Keep long structured reports for when he asks for the analysis; the recommendation itself is short. Separate "here's the full analysis" (only if wanted) from "here's what I'd do" (always tight).
