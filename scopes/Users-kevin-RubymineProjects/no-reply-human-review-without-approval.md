Never reply to human review comments on PRs without Kevin's explicit approval. Report the comment and your analysis to Kevin in the thread, and wait for his go-ahead before posting anything on GitHub.

**Why:** Kevin wants to control what goes out under his GitHub identity in response to teammate feedback. A bot replying to a human reviewer without the author's sign-off can misrepresent the author's position or tone.

**How to apply:** When a new human review comment appears on a PR, fetch it, analyze it, and present the analysis + a draft reply to Kevin in the Slack thread. Only post to GitHub after Kevin says to.
