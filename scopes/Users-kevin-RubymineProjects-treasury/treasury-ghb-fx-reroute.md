Pattern for an FX wire that must go out via GHB: FUNDS_MIGRATION active SA → GHB SA on the CCA (IVC: the IVA rides on the CCA's subs), token `GHB-FX-REROUTE-OW-<id>`, then `ow.update!(sub_account_id: ghb)`. Mirrors `route_through_svb!` args.
- Repointing to GHB also stops auto `route_through_svb!` at submit (`treasury_prime/outgoing_wires/state_service.rb:41`, only fires for piermont/fifth_third).
- Submit book-transfers wire → settlement GA (GHB) from `ow.sub_account`; the GHB SA must hold the cash at TP first.
- **Point the wire back to the active SA before Confirm and Complete.** Excess refund goes settlement → CA active SA (`transfers/create_service.rb:97`), then on completion wire → CA from `ow.sub_account` (`treasury_prime/transfers/state_service.rb:158`), which is a second NT from the emptied GHB SA. A short book-transfers from the empty GHB SA.
- Inactive-SA sweep: auto-forward skips FUNDS_MIGRATION (`transfers/v2/state_service.rb:357`); balance-check auto-heal runs 00:05/08:05/16:05 UTC and only if the active SA's check fails. Fund just before approval.
- The ledger fix (edit `from_sub_account_id` on provisioning LEs) is only needed when the GHB reserve paid (ARP) and the wire was repointed before provisioning. When the wire is provisioned on the active SA and a real FUNDS_MIGRATION moves the cash, the books match the cash.
- An incident report may name the wrong bank. Check prod.
- **Network transfers are not instant on every lane.** Prod, 30 days to 2026-10-20 (created→updated): Piermont SD → GHB Main median 15.9h; FTB → GHB median 1.0h. Measure the lane before quoting a sweep window; I told Kevin "minutes" for Piermont → GHB and he corrected it.
- **INC-5850 (2026-10-28), 3rd reroute for CA 439790 in 15 days:** RCA + 2 scripts at https://arti.internal.angellist.com/s/inc-5850-ghb-reroute-rca/1. **The balance check is CA-level internal vs the ACTIVE sub's TP** (`difference_service.rb:64-70`), and the pending-wire add-back counts only wires on the active sub (`balance_service.rb:390`). So repointing a provisioned wire drops the add-back, and the check stays failed until cash leaves the inactive sub. Editing the provisioning LE's from_sub (from == to) changes nothing for the check; I wrongly claimed it did. No-reserve (self FUNDS_MIGRATION NT, created together with the repoint) should keep the check passing; that is unverified, since it assumes TP debits the origin at NT submit. Fund exactly the provisioned amount: any buffer gets auto-healed at the next :05.
- **INC-5850 outcome (2026-10-29): the no-reserve variant failed.** NT T309760 landed on 495715 at ~12:04 UTC. 498080's 16:24 check then failed on an unrelated +$15,000 (BC 233257), and auto-heal swept the whole $34,215 back (T310164, `bcr:233257`). Auto-heal takes the ENTIRE positive balance of every inactive sub, whatever the diff is, and the fund's active sub fails checks for unrelated reasons often (10/21–10/23 too). **Rule: never park cash on an inactive sub across a :05 run, however clean my own math looks.** Grace approved at 19:04, and the 3 forward BTs 495715 → GA 14 errored at $0. The fix was an ARP book plus `BookTransfers::CreateService.retry!` in a single run.
