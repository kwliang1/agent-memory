## Judgment
`verify_identity` and `alloy_verify` fail with a 422 `verification_ineligible` when the entity can't be verified. Never go back to a silent `true`/`false`: that is how "Run Alloy verification" on a company did nothing. Venture depends on the 422 (CPTR recipient verification and KYC's related-entity checks). Check an IRA as its individual (`verification_subject`) before reading `ineligibility_reasons`.

Role changes through `Entities::AdminRoleUpdateService` move money in treasury: a verified flip resumes parked sub-account requests, and reopening ownership review unverifies the entity and blocks its payments. So screening there is a money-movement gate. A signatory or grantor must have cleared screening, not merely not failed, because a missing status is auto-built as `unchecked` and the first signatory alone can flip an entity to verified.
